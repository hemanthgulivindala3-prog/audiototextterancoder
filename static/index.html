How the pipeline works

    

      

        
01

        
Format Detection & Conversion

        
Any input format is detected via ffmpeg (not just the extension). The audio is normalised to 16 kHz mono PCM — exactly what Whisper expects.

      

      

        
02

        
Intelligent Chunking

        
Files longer than 10 minutes are split into overlapping chunks (3-second overlap) so no words are lost at boundaries. Chunks are merged and deduplicated.

      

      

        
03

        
Whisper Transcription

        
OpenAI Whisper produces word-level timestamps, confidence scores, and auto-detects language. The model is loaded once and cached for speed.

      

      

        
04

        
Structured Output

        
Results include the full transcript, per-segment timestamps, per-word timing, confidence scores, and audio metadata — ready for downstream use.
